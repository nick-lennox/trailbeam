- **`GET /api/events` on Postgres: a 7-day page in tens of milliseconds** (QV-468). Postgres schema 16 keeps an
  hourly rollup of the steps (`step_rollup`, per hour, queue, service and kind, kept exact by ingest and retention,
  as SQLite's), and changes the spans' unique key to (time, trace ID, span ID), as SQLite's, so a page reads about
  a page of rows. On 1M spans over 7 days (our test machine, p95): the first page went from 557 ms to 81 ms, by
  queue from 478 to 16 ms, by service from 431 to 25 ms, Lambda outcomes from 314 to 34 ms, and the next page
  from 340 to 2 ms. Ingest is 2-3 % slower per export: the rollup's rows. On both stores the first page reads rows
  only of the kinds and times its counts found, and a window ending now counts its current hour from the rollup.
  The upgrade builds the key and the rollup from the stored spans, about 2.5 s per million: stop every older
  replica first. A span sent again with another end time is now stored twice on Postgres, as on SQLite.
