- **RabbitMQ messages get their own timeline** (QV-307, part 3 of 6 of RabbitMQ support; contract 4.0,
  `contract/span-contract-amqp.md` A5 to A10). `/api/messages/~rabbitmq~<message_id>` now says `system: rabbitmq` and
  shows RabbitMQ's steps in RabbitMQ's terms, never "visibility timeout":
  - acks, requeues (`basic.nack` or `basic.reject` with requeue), refusals (requeue = false: dead-lettered or dropped),
    closed channels with their initiator and reply text, and unroutable publishes (`basic.return`) as steps of their own;
  - each redelivery as an inferred "returned to the queue" step with the cause the spans show: requeued, channel
    closed, consumer timeout, the broker cancelled the consumer, or not observed;
  - the move to a dead-letter queue from the `x-death` header, with its reason (`rejected`, `expired`, `maxlen`,
    `delivery_limit`) and its first time;
  - a "routed" step after the publish listing the queues the message reached (a lower bound), one copy per queue;
  - notices for a message published twice with one `message_id` (`published_twice`) and for a `basic.nack` requeue
    loop (`requeue_loop`, from 3 requeues: since RabbitMQ 4.3 they never reach `x-delivery-limit`).
  - Stuck and Duplicates count per queue copy, as before; a refused copy and an unroutable message are no longer
    listed as stuck. Receive counts from different headers are not compared to order deliveries (A9).
  - **`delivery_limit`** in the config file sets a RabbitMQ queue's `x-delivery-limit`, so a message whose
    `x-delivery-count` passed it shows "not seen on a dead-letter queue yet".
  - On Postgres a broker cancel is not read yet, so that cause shows as not observed there.
  - **The built-in web screen** uses RabbitMQ's words on a RabbitMQ message's page: published to an exchange,
    routed, delivered, acked, requeued, refused, returned to the queue with its cause, and the `x-death` move with
    its reason, shown as reported by RabbitMQ. The page shows each queue's copy, and the ID as the application set
    it. The Duplicates and Stuck pages explain per-copy counting.
  - **Stuck causes** (`/api/causes`): a delivered, unacked RabbitMQ message is in flight with its consumer (no
    visibility timeout applies), labelled "has the delivery and hasn't acked it (basic.ack)".
  - **Queue pages and flows** count a RabbitMQ publish on each queue it reached instead of on the exchange, and
    read dead-letter moves from `x-death`. A requeue is not counted as a visibility change. For messages stored
    before this version, the queue events stay as they were written.
