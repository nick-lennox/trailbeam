- **Dashboard measures shared by both products** (QV-591, One UI 13, part 1, `@trailbeam/ui` 0.1.0-dev.26). A new
  entry, `@trailbeam/ui/dashboards`, has a dashboard's definition (variables, sections and widgets, and the
  starters) and the widget measures engine, moved from the hosted app. `dashboardMeasures()` makes the engine over
  a query language and, on the hosted app, its sample figures. It gives the same answers as the server's engine on
  all the cases in `contract/fixtures/measures-parity.json`. `fmt` now lives in one place for the charts and the
  measures. Nothing on a page changes yet.
