- **Upgrading from 0.4.0 on Postgres: stop every 0.4.0 replica before the first 0.5.0 server starts** (QV-720).
  0.5.0 upgrades the database from Postgres schema 6 to 23. A 0.4.0 replica left running keeps storing spans,
  with no error, in the tables it knows only: those spans never reach the queue pages, flows and `/api/events`,
  and nothing adds them later. Timelines and `/api/stats` still have them. In our test, 45 s of mixed running at
  100 messages/s left 4,339 of 47,358 sent messages off the queue pages. **Helm:** the chart's default rolling
  update runs old and new pods together, so upgrade with `--set strategy.type=Recreate`, or scale the Deployment
  to 0 first. The chart now leaves out `rollingUpdate` when the type is `Recreate`, as Kubernetes requires, and its
  notes say this. Ingest is down until a new pod is ready.
