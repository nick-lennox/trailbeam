section: Fixed
---

- **A purge no longer marks same-named queues in other accounts or regions purged** (QV-725). Purges were matched
  to messages by queue name alone, so a `PurgeQueue` on `orders` in one account or region showed messages on
  another account's `orders` as "Purged" and took them off the Stuck list and the queue's "In the queue now". A
  purge is now matched by queue key (account, region and name), as the rest of the server tells queues apart. A
  purge whose queue URL names no region (an emulator's) still applies to the one queue it fits. Upgrading adds the
  key to every stored message (SQLite schema 32, Postgres schema 26), read from its spans.
