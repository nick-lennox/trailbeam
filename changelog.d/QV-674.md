- **An alert check stopped mid-way no longer loses its alerts** (QV-674, internal until alerts ship in QV-592). The
  check wrote the new state before sending, so a shutdown, a crash or a failed delivery in between left those
  changes "already sent", and no later check sent them. Now the changes wait in a new `alert_outbox` table (SQLite
  schema 29, Postgres schema 23) until delivery returns, and the next check sends what a stopped one left: at least
  once, tried up to 3 times, for up to 6 hours, and an error is logged when it gives up. `stop()` waits a bounded
  8 s (under `docker stop`'s 10 s). A check that runs past 5 minutes is logged as an error, and `/api/health` has a
  new `alert_check` field: `late` when no check has ended for two intervals, `stuck` past the deadline, `failing`
  after an error. The alerts API keeps rules and channels in the store's tables, not in memory, links to
  `--public-url`, and sends email through the server's mail settings (`TRAILBEAM_SMTP_HOST`).
