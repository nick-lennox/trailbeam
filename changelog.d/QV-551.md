- **Node.js counts the 256-character tenant limit in code points** (QV-551). A tenant made of characters outside
  the Basic Multilingual Plane, such as emoji, counted two per character, so a tenant of 129 to 256 of them was
  dropped by Node.js and kept by Python, Java and .NET. All four packages now keep it, on both the
  message-attribute and the body-path branch. New `capture` tenant vectors cover 200, 256 and 257 emoji.
