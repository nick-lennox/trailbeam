section: Changed
---

- **The queue page has the hosted app's design** (QV-584). `/queue?name=<queue>` is drawn in the new frame: the
  sidebar, dark by default with a light toggle, and the top bar's search. The queue's name is a serif heading under an
  Overview breadcrumb; its settings and the "Now" figures are in two panels of figures; each chart, the selected
  minutes, what Trailbeam can't see, the senders, the consumers and why messages didn't finish are panels with the
  hosted titles. Notes that were browser `title` hovers (the span-only verdict, why a setting is not known, a deploy
  marker) are now the app's own hover notes; a deploy marker's note is in the chart's hover box. Link arrows and
  "a · b" fragments are gone: a Lambda trigger reads "Trigger: batch size 10, batching window 5 s, …". Nothing the page
  showed before is lost, the range picker, the selected minutes, By version and the JSON link work as before, and the
  hosted app draws the same page from `@trailbeam/ui`.
