section: Changed
---

- **Only a browser sees an invite or reset link** (QV-712). With an admin's personal API token or a sign-in
  through the proxy, `POST /api/users` still adds the person and emails the invite, but leaves out `link.url`, and
  `POST /api/users/{id}/link` only emails the link (otherwise `403`, `reason` `needs_a_session`). A link signs in
  whoever opens it, so an admin's token could otherwise reset its own person's password, or add a new admin and take
  their invite, and add a passkey that keeps signing in after the token is revoked.
