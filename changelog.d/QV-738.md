---
section: Changed
---

- **Clearer words on Dashboards, Messages, Alerts, Triage, AWS settings, Tokens and People** (QV-738). The
  dashboard measures are now "Handling time, p95", "Handling errors", "Dead-letter queue depth" and "One receive
  from the dead-letter queue", in the UI and in the server's measures. The Messages list's Handled column has its
  own note, and a message due on the dead-letter queue reads "reached maxReceiveCount; the move to the dead-letter
  queue isn't seen yet". An alert rule's "Back after 6 h quiet" is now "New messages arrive after none for 6 h" in
  the list and the form, a channel's Send select is "Alert levels", and a mute with no end says "Nothing is sent
  until it is unmuted". "Ignored for 4 more" is "Ignored for 4 more messages". The AWS settings say a read problem
  once, and "Not reading" when reads are on but no queue is named. A token's prefix has no ellipsis, and the People
  buttons say "Make a reset link" and "Make a new invite link".
