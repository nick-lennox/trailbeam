---
section: Changed
---

- **Timeline sentences use SQS's words** (QV-746). An SQS step is now named after its receive: "the delete of
  receive 2", "the visibility change of receive 1", "the Lambda outcome of receive 1". The inferred redelivery's
  reason ends "so the message was received again", the `dlq_move_not_seen` notice says SQS moves the message
  "instead of making it visible again", and the `key_mismatch` and `unmatched_handle` notices say "receive" in
  place of "delivery". RabbitMQ steps still say "of delivery N", RabbitMQ's word. "The lambda outcome" at the
  start of a sentence is now "The Lambda outcome".
