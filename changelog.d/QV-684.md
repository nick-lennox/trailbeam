- **Dashboards: limits on what one measures request costs** (QV-684, part of QV-591). A dashboard holds at most 50
  widgets, a widget at most 5 queries and a queue table at most 19 columns. A query's lines are the largest 10 and
  one `Other (k)` line for the rest, as the hosted dashboard draws them. Once `GET /api/dashboards/{id}/measures`
  has 8 MB of JSON or has taken 5 seconds, the widgets left come back with `skipped`, to be asked for one at a time
  (`/widgets/{wid}/measures`). Before, a widget grouped by `message_id` was a line per message: 2 MB and 0.14
  seconds per widget, so a dashboard of many such widgets took minutes and gigabytes per request.
