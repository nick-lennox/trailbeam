- **RabbitMQ: a forged `x-death` no longer hides a stuck message** (QV-527; contract A8). RabbitMQ passes a
  publisher's own `x-death` and `x-first-death-*` headers through unchanged, so anyone who could publish to a queue
  an instrumented consumer reads, under a known message ID, could make a delivery report a dead-letter move off
  another queue. That closed the copy there and took the message off Stuck. Trailbeam now takes `x-death` as a move
  off a queue only when the message's latest delivery there was refused, requeued or its channel closed, or no
  delivery there was seen. When that delivery is still held by its consumer or was acked, there is no move: the copy
  stays open (and on Stuck), and the timeline gets the new notice `x_death_not_a_move`.
