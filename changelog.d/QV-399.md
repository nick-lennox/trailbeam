- **Event explorer** (QV-399). A new Events page (`/events`) lists every recorded step (send, receive, delete,
  visibility change, Lambda outcome) in a time range, newest first, from `GET /api/events`, with a chart of steps
  per minute, hour or day and their counts by kind. Filter with `queue:`, `service:`, `kind:` (and `-kind:`) and
  `message_id:`; click a bar, or drag across several, to list that span; click a queue or service to filter on it.
  The query and range live in the URL, with Copy link and Refresh every 15 s as on the lists. Export writes the
  matching steps, up to the newest 10,000, as CSV. The view is `EventExplorer` in `@trailbeam/ui`.
