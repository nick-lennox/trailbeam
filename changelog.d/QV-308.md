- **RabbitMQ in the app's views** (QV-308, part 4 of 6 of RabbitMQ support). In `@trailbeam/ui`, the views both
  products share:
  - **The message page** of a RabbitMQ message has a RabbitMQ badge, shows the ID as the application set it (without
    the `~rabbitmq~` mark), and names each step in RabbitMQ's terms: published to an exchange, routed, delivered,
    acked, requeued, refused, channel closed, returned to the publisher (`basic.return`), returned to the queue with
    its cause, and dead-lettered as the `x-death` header reports it, with its reason. The heading, the state ("Done",
    "No ack seen") and the figures ("Deliveries, acked") use the same words.
  - **One row per queue copy**: the page lists each queue's copy of the message with its deliveries and how it ended,
    so a fanout to two queues is one message with two rows, not a duplicate.
  - SQS messages' pages are unchanged.
