---
section: Changed
---

- **Shared design parts follow the design and copy rules** (QV-657, `@trailbeam/ui` 0.1.0-dev.18, internal until
  the pages move onto it). TopBar and PageHeader no longer say "Updated 4s ago" unless the caller asks: `fresh` is
  off by default, `fresh={<time>…</time>}` shows the real time ("Updated 12s ago"), and `fresh` alone keeps the
  sample pages' fixed mark. Both page headings are serif. The default Logo and Mark take the theme's ink and
  accent, so they show in the night app as well as on a day page (`tone="sky"` stays for the landing page). Button
  `inverse` has dark text on its light fill in both themes (it was light on light under night). Source tags are no
  longer mono, and read "Trailbeam package" and "Function logs". `purgeText`, and the StatePill notes built on it,
  read "Purged by a PurgeQueue call at 12:00:09.000 UTC" and "Possibly purged: a PurgeQueue call ran at
  12:00:09.000 UTC".
