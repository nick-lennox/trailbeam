- **RabbitMQ: publisher-set receive counts are ignored** (QV-529; contract A7). RabbitMQ passes `x-acquired-count`
  and `x-delivery-count` headers set by the publisher through to the first delivery, on classic and quorum queues,
  and the pika and amqplib hooks trusted them, so a forged count showed deliveries not observed and a return to the
  queue after an acked delivery, and a forged `x-delivery-count` could reach the `x-delivery-limit` warning. A
  delivery with `redelivered = false` and no usable `x-death` now always reports receive count 1, source
  `first_delivery`, and omits `trailbeam.amqp.acquired_count` and `trailbeam.amqp.delivery_count`.
