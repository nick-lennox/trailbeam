section: Changed
---

- **Asking before what can't be undone, one way everywhere** (QV-739). Removing a passkey, disabling someone,
  and making yourself a member on People now ask first, in the row, as removing someone and revoking a token
  did; before, they happened at once, and making yourself a member sent you to the Overview without a word.
  Deleting an alert, removing an alert channel and deleting a dashboard from the list now ask in the app's own
  theme instead of the browser's grey box. The dashboard dialog's backdrop follows the theme (it was a fixed navy
  on the day theme too), the Live and Paused button is one part on the Event explorer, Messages and dashboards,
  and a widget with its own time range says so in its clock mark's tooltip. `MessagesTable`, which no page drew
  any more, is gone from `@trailbeam/ui`, with `rowFacts` and the `Column` type: use `MessagesView`.
