- **An S3 notification without an `eTag` no longer joins a recorded upload** (QV-565; contract revision 3.7, section
  27.3). Anyone allowed to `SendMessage` to a captured queue could post an `ObjectCreated` record of their own with
  the bucket, the key and a time near a recorded upload, and its message showed that upload as its sender and was
  listed among the messages the upload reached. S3 writes `eTag` in every such record, so the server now joins a
  receive only when it has the eTag the upload has. A record made by hand that copies the upload's ETag (from S3's own
  notification, `HeadObject`, or the MD5 of the bytes) is still joined; section 27.3 says so.
