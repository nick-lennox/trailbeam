section: Changed
---

- **Duplicates (`/duplicates`) is the shared Messages list on `GET /api/duplicates`** (QV-586, `@trailbeam/ui`
  0.1.0-dev.30). It is in the app frame with the Event explorer's query bar, time picker, results bar, Columns, Export
  and rows, as Messages is. Filter with `queue:` and `reason:` (`received_more_than_once` or
  `succeeded_more_than_once`) in one line; a term the list can't take is named before anything is asked. The Why
  column still says each message's count ("received 3 times, handled 2 times"), and the range, Live and Copy link work
  as on Messages. Links from the old page (`reason`, `queue`, `range=24h`, `from_ms` and `to_ms`, `refresh`, `limit`,
  `cursor`) still open the same rows. `@trailbeam/ui` adds `WHY_COLUMN`, `LIST_REASONS`, `duplicateWhyText` and a
  `reason` field for `messageQueryLanguage`.
