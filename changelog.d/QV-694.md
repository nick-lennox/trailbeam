section: Fixed
---

- **A passkey with an over-long credential ID is refused** (QV-694). Adding a passkey whose credential ID is over
  1023 bytes (WebAuthn Level 3, section 7.1) or whose public key is over 2048 bytes now gets `400`, as does one whose
  `rawId` is not the credential ID the authenticator attested. Before, such a passkey was saved and could never sign
  in, and on Postgres a long enough one was a `500`.
