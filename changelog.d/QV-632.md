- **Dashboard widget measures engine in the server** (QV-632, internal until dashboards ship in QV-591):
  `trailbeam.server.measures` works out what a dashboard widget shows (event counts, rates, p95 times, queue depth,
  age, headroom and time to drain, by group and time bucket, with the widget's filter and variables) and checks a
  dashboard definition, with the hosted app's behaviour, held to it by shared cases in
  `contract/fixtures/measures-parity.json` (shipped as `@trailbeam/ui/fixtures/measures-parity.json`, 0.1.0-dev.14);
  nothing calls it yet.
