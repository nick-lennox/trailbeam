- **Dashboard starters have data on a server that does not read AWS** (QV-772, internal until dashboards ship in
  QV-591). With AWS reads off, the default, the Queue health starter used to open with 9 of its 12 widgets saying
  "No data" (queue depth, age of the oldest message, DLQ depth and headroom all come from AWS). Now each starter on
  such a server is built from what the spans record: sent, received and deleted per minute, time in queue and
  processing time (p95), processing errors and messages dead-lettered, per queue and over time. A note at the top
  says how an admin turns AWS reads on. One queue in depth starts on every queue, not the hosted sample queue
  `email-dispatch`. With AWS reads on, and on the hosted app, the starters are unchanged.
