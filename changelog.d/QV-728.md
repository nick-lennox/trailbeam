- **No "producer is not instrumented" notice under a joined SNS publish, PutEvents entry or S3 upload** (QV-728).
  The message page of a message SNS, EventBridge or S3 delivered, whose upstream span Trailbeam joined
  (`published`, `put_event`, `s3_upload`), no longer says "No trailbeam send span for this message: the producer is
  not instrumented". The producer is instrumented and on the page; the SQS send was the AWS service's own. A message
  whose upstream step is only inferred from its body (no recorded publish, entry or upload) still shows the notice.
