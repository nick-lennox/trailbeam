- **Passkeys** (QV-665). People can sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, a phone or a security key:
  **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in page, and a new **Account** page (open it from your name at the top)
  that lists your passkeys, adds one and removes one. API: `/api/me/passkeys`. Off unless the server has its public
  URL (`TRAILBEAM_PUBLIC_URL`) and the new extra: `pip install "trailbeam[server,passkeys]"`. Each passkey must
  check the person (user verification); a counter that goes back is refused; failed passkey sign-ins count against
  the address's sign-in limit. New tables `passkeys` and `passkey_challenges` (SQLite schema 28, Postgres schema 22),
  made empty on upgrade. `/api/health` says whether passkeys are on (`config.passkeys`).
