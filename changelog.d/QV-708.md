section: Changed
---

- **Stuck is now Issues** (QV-708). The web screen's Stuck page is replaced by Issues at `/issues`, the same page
  as the hosted app's: one row per cause with its messages drawn along one time range, filters down the left, and
  a page for each issue at `/issues/<cause ID>` with where on the flow it stops messages, what its messages have
  in common, the timeline of one of them and the messages themselves. The list has one time control, its range;
  how old an undeleted message must be to count is the server's `--stuck-after`, or `?age=` in a link (`15m`,
  `1h`). The dead-letter queues, redrive and the redrive log are under the list, and each issue has Alert me while
  the alert check is on. `/stuck` links, alert links among them, open the matching Issues page, with their
  queue, age, range and cause. The sidebar item and the Overview's "Stuck on <queue>" links say Issues. Without
  the web screen's build, `/stuck` and `/issues` are the server's HTML page as before.
