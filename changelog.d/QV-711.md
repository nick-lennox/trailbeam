---
section: Changed
---

- **One time range per page, with the same names everywhere** (QV-711). The Tenants page and a queue's page now
  use the Event explorer's time picker ("Past 1 hour", "Past 1 day", ..., or a typed or exact range), in place of
  Tenants' "Last hour / Last 24 hours / Last 7 days" buttons and the queue page's drop-down. Tenants keeps its range
  in the URL as `range=` (or `from=` and `to=`), as every other page does. An older `/tenants?since_s=3600` link
  still opens the same range and is rewritten to `range=1h`. The Event explorer now opens on the past hour, like the
  Overview and the hosted explorer, rather than on a day that drew as a single bar. Dragging over the explorer's
  chart, or clicking a bar, now sets the page's time range in the time picker, rather than adding a second
  "from to" range with its own clear button. The chart's hover note is the design system's tooltip.
  `@trailbeam/ui` 0.1.0-dev.31: `TenantsPage` takes the host's range control as `control` (in place of `ranges`
  and `range`), `EventExplorer` takes `setTime`, `EventChart` no longer takes `win`, and `tenantsTime`,
  `tenantsHref` and `tenantsUpgrade` read and write the Tenants range for both products.
