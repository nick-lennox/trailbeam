section: Changed
---

- **The message page has the hosted app's design** (QV-581). `/message?id=<message ID>` is drawn in the new frame: the
  sidebar, dark by default with a light toggle, and the top bar's search. A breadcrumb (Messages, the queue, the
  message) sits over the one-sentence answer as a serif heading, then the state, who received it, its notices, and the
  Timeline and Body tabs. The timeline takes the design system's colors and type: values such as times and IDs are in
  the mono face, labels never are. Where a step came from and what proved an order are explained in the app's own
  hover notes, not browser `title` hovers. "a · b" fragments and arrows are gone: a step's facts read "Delivery 2,
  Receive count 2", a dead-letter move reads "From orders To orders-dlq". Copy message ID and Copy link are on the
  page. Nothing the page showed before is lost: the Body tab, the JSON links and the error causes at the foot work as
  before, and the hosted app draws the same page from `@trailbeam/ui`.
