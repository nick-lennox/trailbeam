- **An alert is no longer lost when a store write fails just after the check records it** (QV-757). The scheduled
  alert check now puts the changes it won in the outbox as soon as it has recorded them, before the "Alert me" rules'
  own writes. Before, a busy SQLite file or a Postgres blip while noting a regression or clearing a snooze failed the
  workspace and dropped every other alert of that check without a log line, and a check stopped at that moment did
  the same. Now the other alerts are sent, the failed write is logged and tried again at the next check, and a
  stopped check's alerts are sent by the next one.
