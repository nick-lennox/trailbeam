---
section: Changed
---

- **The People and Tokens pages have the hosted app's design** (QV-666, part of QV-587). Both move into the new
  frame: the sidebar, dark by default with a light toggle, and the top bar's search. People is a list as the hosted
  app's members are: each person's name, email, role, state and last sign-in, with the same actions as before (role,
  new invite or password reset link, disable or enable, remove with its confirmation). A state's explanation is a
  hover note on the state, and "The only admin who can sign in" is written in that person's row instead of a hover
  note on the controls that are off. Tokens keeps both sections, the open-ingest warning and its confirmation, the
  legacy shared token's line, expiry choices and the token shown once. Nothing either page showed is gone.
- **`@trailbeam/ui` 0.1.0-dev.21**: the People and Tokens parts (`PeopleTable`, `AddPerson`, `OneTimeLink`,
  `TokensTable`, `CreateToken`, `OneTimeToken`) move to `@trailbeam/ui/design`, beside new shared settings parts
  (`SettingsSection`, `Field`, `SelectField`, `StateMark`, `Problem`, `Caution`, `OneTimeBox`). Their words stay in the
  main entry.
- **The first-run screen has the hosted app's design** (QV-666). While nothing is stored, `/` is in the new frame,
  with its three steps, the runtime switch, the code to copy (now in the app's dark code window, in both themes)
  and the waiting line as before. Its short lines joined by dots are sentences now: "Setup for Python, sending to ...",
  and "m-1 on orders, received by orders-worker." Once the first message is in, the Messages list still shows below
  it without a reload, in a dark panel until the list itself moves to the new design.
- **A new Account page at `/account`**, for anyone signed in: who you are signed in as, and the ways you can sign in
  (your password, and your single sign-on or GitHub account with when you last used it, marked Off when this server
  no longer offers it). Your name in the sidebar and in the header opens it. It is built from the hosted app's
  account card, now shared as `AccountCard` in `@trailbeam/ui/design`, which also lists passkeys where the server
  has them.
- **`/api/me` says how you can sign in** (`sign_in`: whether you have a password, and your accounts at an identity
  provider), for the Account page.
- **`@trailbeam/ui` 0.1.0-dev.24**: `FirstRun`, `CodeWindow` and `highlight` move to `@trailbeam/ui/design` (the
  install commands, examples and `environment` stay in the main entry), and `AccountCard` is new there, with its
  words (`accountWays`, `deviceName`) in the main entry.
- **Sign in, Create your admin account and Choose your password have the hosted app's design** (QV-666). They
  sit in the hosted sign-in's pre-dawn sky, with the logo, a serif heading, labels above the fields, the single
  sign-on, GitHub and passkey buttons above the form, and what went wrong in a tinted box. The access token page,
  Sign-in failed and Not allowed match them. They still work without scripts and fetch nothing: each is one
  response, as before. Every sentence, field and button they had is kept. The password length is now written under
  the field ("At least 10 characters."). Instead of the old header's links, Not allowed links to Messages, your
  account and Sign out, and the footer still gives the version and the server's health.
