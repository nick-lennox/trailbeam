section: Changed
---

- **Changelog entries are now one file per pull request** (QV-448). A pull request adds `changelog.d/QV-NNN.md`
  with its entry and its section (Added, Changed or Fixed) instead of a line under Unreleased here, and
  `scripts/changelog.py build` folds the files in at release. CI's *Changelog* job fails a pull request that adds
  a line under Unreleased directly. Pull requests that were already open merge as they are.
